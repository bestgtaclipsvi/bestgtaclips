@bestgtaclips.vi · TikTok · Instagram · YouTube

BestGTAClips publishes short, edited highlight clips of Grand Theft Auto VI gameplay: funny moments, reactions and the best plays, with subtitles in Spanish and full credit to the creator of the original footage.

BestGTAClips Uploader is our desktop tool. We use it to send the clips we finish editing to the drafts inbox of our own TikTok account (TikTok Content Posting API, upload mode). Nothing is posted automatically: each video arrives as a notification in the TikTok app, where we review it, write the caption, choose who can see it and publish it ourselves.

Credits and creators

Every clip shows the name of the creator whose stream it comes from, on screen and in the description. If you are a creator and want a clip removed, write to us and we take it down within 48 hours.
