Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains what data BestGTAClips Uploader (“the app”, “we”) handles when you connect a TikTok account.

1. What we collect

  • TikTok account basics (scope user.info.basic): your open ID, display name and avatar, only to show which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, so the app can upload videos to your drafts inbox (scope video.upload).
  • Upload status returned by TikTok (for example “sent to inbox” or “published”).

We do not collect your password, contacts, messages, location, followers, comments or any data about other users.

2. How we use it

Only to upload the videos you choose to your own TikTok drafts inbox and to show you the status of each upload. The app never posts publicly on its own: you finish and publish every video inside the TikTok app.

3. Where it is stored

All of this data stays on the computer where the app runs, in a file readable only by that computer’s user. We do not send it to our own servers or to third parties, and we do not sell or share it. Videos are sent only to TikTok, through TikTok’s official API.

4. How long we keep it

Until you disconnect. Pressing “Disconnect” in the app revokes the TikTok permission and deletes the stored tokens and account details. You can also remove the app at any time from TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.

5. Children

The app is not directed to anyone under 18.

6. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above.
